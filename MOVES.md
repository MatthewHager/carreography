# Move catalogue

What each move looks like, when the choreographer plays it, and what it locks to. Every move lights the whole lineup and grows with the music (see docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md). Written for the classic Model Y | Cybertruck | Model Y lineup: in any lineup "the truck" is the centre Cybertruck and "the Ys" are the other cars.

| move | what you see | plays | locks to |
|---|---|---|---|
| keyboard | notes play across the stage like a keyboard, low on the crowd's left and high on the right, the melody along the truck's bar | a passage that follows its melody | note |
| relay | one light runs the stage note by note, through every lamp and along the bar, turning when the melody turns | a passage that follows its melody | note |
| mirror | pitch is the distance from the middle: low notes light the centre, high notes both ends, so a rising line opens out and a falling one closes in | a passage that follows its melody | note |
| lr_notes | notes alternate between the left and right halves of the stage | a passage that follows its melody | note |
| antiphony | call and response: bar by bar the melody passes between the left and the right half of the stage | a passage that follows its melody | note |
| block | chords as blocks of light from their lowest note to their highest, held with the chord; a big chord fills the stage | every phrase shaped as chords | note |
| shimmer | a trill flickers between the two halves of a car, and across every car when loud | every phrase shaped as trill | self |
| motif | the signature motif: an ornament on the middle car, a sweep across, a hold where it lands | every phrase shaped as motif | self |
| cadence | a big closing chord: the whole stage on for the chord | every phrase shaped as cadence | self |
| march | one light marches car to car on the beat, left, centre, right, centre | a passage that follows its beat | beat |
| lr_channel | left channel, right channel: the whole left half of the stage on one beat, the right on the next | a passage that follows its beat | beat |
| checker | a checkerboard: every other car on one beat, the others on the next, the bar in blocks | a passage that follows its beat | beat |
| tug_of_war | the light fills the stage slot by slot on the beat, crowd-left to right, then drains back | a passage that follows its beat | beat |
| converge | every beat the light runs in from both ends and meets in the middle | a passage that follows its beat | beat |
| beam | a searchlight two slots wide sweeps the stage over each bar, and back the next | a passage that follows its beat | bar |
| shockwave | on each downbeat (each beat from drive up) a ring of light leaves the middle and runs out to both ends | a passage that follows its beat | bar |
