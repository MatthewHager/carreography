# Move catalogue

What each move looks like, when the choreographer plays it, and what it locks to. Every move lights the whole lineup and grows with the music (see docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md). Written for the classic Model Y | Cybertruck | Model Y lineup: in any lineup "the truck" is the centre Cybertruck and "the Ys" are the other cars.

| move | what you see | plays | locks to |
|---|---|---|---|
| keyboard | notes play across the stage like a keyboard, low on the crowd's left and high on the right, the melody along the truck's bar | a figure that follows its melody | note |
| relay | one light runs the stage note by note, through every lamp and along the bar, end to end and back | a figure that follows its melody | note |
| mirror | pitch is the distance from the middle: low notes light the centre, high notes both ends, so a rising line opens out and a falling one closes in | a figure that follows its melody | note |
| antiphony | call and response: bar by bar the melody passes between the left and the right half of the stage | a figure that follows its melody | note |
| block | chords as blocks of light from their lowest note to their highest, held with the chord; a big chord fills the stage | every phrase shaped as chords | note |
| shimmer | a trill flickers between the two halves of a car, and across every car when loud | every phrase shaped as trill | self |
| motif | the signature motif: an ornament on the middle car, a sweep across, a hold where it lands | every phrase shaped as motif | self |
| cadence | a big closing chord: the whole stage on for the chord | every phrase shaped as cadence | self |
| swell | held chords with no beat to hit: the whole stage swells from the middle car out and holds while the chord sounds, breathing out and in again from the middle at each new chord | a chord held with no beat to follow | self |
| march | one light marches car to car on the beat, left, centre, right, centre | a figure that follows its beat, below the peak | beat |
| lr_channel | left channel, right channel: the whole left half of the stage on one beat, the right on the next | a figure that follows its beat | beat |
| checker | a checkerboard: every other car on one beat, the others on the next, the bar in blocks | a figure that follows its beat | beat |
| tug_of_war | the light fills the stage slot by slot on the beat, from one end, then drains back; the next time from the other end | a figure that follows its beat | beat |
| converge | on the beat the light runs in from both ends and meets in the middle on the "and" | a figure that follows its beat | beat |
| beam | a searchlight two slots wide sweeps the stage over each bar, and back the next | a figure that follows its beat, below the peak | bar |
| shockwave | on the beat a ring of light leaves the middle and runs out to both ends, the stage held lit until the next | a figure that follows its beat | beat |
| glide | the melody glides across the inner beams by pitch, low on the crowd's left, a spot along the bar; a beam it rests on swells in | a figure where the music flows | note |
| bloom | the melody glides out from the middle as it rises, on the inner beams at both ends at once, two spots along the bar | a figure where the music flows | note |
