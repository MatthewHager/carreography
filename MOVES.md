# Move catalogue

What each move looks like, the moods and phrase shapes the choreographer may use it for, and what it locks to. Written for the classic Model Y | Cybertruck | Model Y lineup: in any lineup "the truck" is the centre Cybertruck and "the Ys" are the other cars (see docs/CHOREOGRAPHY.md).

| move | what you see | moods | fits | locks to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| keyboard | notes play across the stage like a keyboard, low on the crowd's left, high on the right | calm, groove, drive, frenzy | run, figuration | note |
| relay | one light runs the stage note by note - left Y, along the bar, right Y - with a short trail | calm, groove, drive | run, figuration | note |
| lr_notes | each note jumps between the left and right halves of the stage | groove, drive, frenzy | figuration, trill | note |
| rotation | notes rotate round the six stage slots, a spinning wheel of light | drive, frenzy | figuration | note |

| pulse | slow notes glow where they sit in pitch, with a soft comet on the bar | calm, groove | recitative | note |
| block | chords as solid blocks of light, held as long as the chord | calm, groove, drive, frenzy | chords, recitative | note |
| rain | notes fall as drops of light at random across the stage | groove, drive, frenzy | trill, figuration | note |
